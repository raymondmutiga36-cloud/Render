🌾 About ShambaLink

ShambaLink is a digital platform designed to connect farmers, buyers, and transporters in Kenya.

Many farmers struggle to find reliable markets, which leads to food waste and low income. ShambaLink aims to solve this problem by providing a simple system where farmers can list produce, buyers can find fresh goods, and transporters can connect to delivery opportunities.

The goal is to reduce food waste, increase farmer income, and improve food distribution across the country.
