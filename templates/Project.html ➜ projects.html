🌾 ShambaLink

Status: In Development

ShambaLink is a digital platform connecting farmers, buyers, and transporters in Kenya to reduce food waste and increase farmer income.

📍 Launch Plan

  • Start in Mombasa
  • Expand to Nairobi

🛠 Development Roadmap

  • Version 1: Basic listing, transport request, simple rating
  • Version 2: GPS tracking, reliability score
  • Version 3: Quality inspection system

🌍 Vision

To build Africa’s most trusted agricultural marketplace and increase income opportunities for farmers across the country.
